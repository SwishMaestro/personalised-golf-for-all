Three design directions

Test pages for Phase 1. Each shows the home page, one question screen and one result card with placeholder content. Open each on laptop and phone, then decide which to keep, mix or change.

  • A · Yardage Book

    Editorial and minimal. Cream paper, thin lines, numbered sections, a serif like a printed magazine.

    Mainly from brandonyasin.com

  • B · Members' Card

    Bold and premium. Dark green, huge condensed type, brass details, results as cards, more motion.

    Mainly from moto-card.com

  • C · Morning Round

    Calm and luxurious. Big photos, lots of space, an elegant light serif, slow fades.

    Mainly from tengilemalamala.com
