How it works

From the first tee to your shortlist

Each answer is one shot. By the time the ball drops, your results are ready.

STEP 01

Play 10 questions

Your hand, budget, level, usual miss and what matters most. "Not sure" is always an answer.

STEP 02

Filter, then score

Irons that can never fit, like the wrong hand or far over budget, are taken out. The rest are scored on what you need.

Felfernig et al., 2015 ASQ

STEP 03

Get your top 3

Each with at least two reasons linked to your answers, a trade-off and an honest next step.

About this project

A student project, built from real research DRAFT

Personalised Golf for All is Leon's MYP Personal Project: learning how a golfer's game and an iron's design fit together, and how to weigh the evidence, then turning it into a free tool.

The questionnaire plays one hole, inspired by the 15th at Crans-sur-Sierre, Switzerland, where Ludvig Åberg hit his favourite shot on the way to winning the 2023 Omega European Masters. Source: PGA Tour (opens in a new tab)
