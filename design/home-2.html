How it works

01

Ten questions. One hole.

Your hand, budget, level, usual miss and what matters most. Each answer is one shot, from the tee to the cup. "Not sure" is always an answer.

02

Filtered. Then scored.

Irons that can never fit, like the wrong hand or far over budget, are taken out. The rest are scored on what you need.1,2

03

Three honest answers.

Your top 3, each with at least two reasons linked to your answers, a trade-off and a next step.

What you get

A shortlist that explains itself.

Every result card has the same four parts, so you always know why.

  1. 1
    Reasons from your answersAt least two, each linked to something you said.

  4. 4

Why you can trust it

Built on research. Honest about limits.

Every fact has a source.

Online tools are only as good as their data and can repeat common myths. Here, every claim links to where it came from.6

Not a fitting. And it says so.

It can't watch your swing, so it can't fit lie, length or shaft, or judge feel and sound. A fitting starts with you hitting balls.6,8

Budget irons included.

In testing, the Takomo 101 matched major brands for ball speed and distance. What you mainly give up is the chance to try before you buy.9

Three options. Not one winner.

A score is only as good as the weights behind it, so you get a shortlist with trade-offs and make the final call.2

About this project Draft

A student project. A real course.

Personalised Golf for All is Leon's MYP Personal Project: learning how a golfer's game and an iron's design fit together, and how to weigh the evidence, then turning it into a free tool.
