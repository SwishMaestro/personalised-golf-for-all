No. 02 How it works

Three steps. One hole.

You answer as you play. Each answer moves the ball one shot closer to the cup, and your results are waiting at the end.

  1. 01

    Play 10 questions

    Your hand, budget, level, usual miss and what matters most to you. "Not sure" is always an answer.

  2. 02

    Filter, then score

    Irons that can never fit, like the wrong hand or far over budget, are taken out. The rest are scored on what you need.

    Felfernig et al., 2015 · ASQ
  3. 03

    Your top 3

    Each with at least two reasons linked to your answers, a trade-off and an honest next step.

No. 03 What you get

A shortlist that explains itself

Every suggestion shows why it fits you, what you give up, and what to do next. Every fact has its source next to it.
